A different way of understanding behavior.

What if the next step in addressing student behavior and educator burnout wasn't another behavior management program — but the nervous system? The Regulated Classroom® is an evidence-based framework that helps educators understand behavior through the lens of the nervous system while intentionally supporting educator regulation, student regulation, connection, and felt safety.

For schools navigating staff stress and burnout, challenging behaviors, absenteeism, and the increasing demands placed on educators, this work offers a different way forward. Participants gain an understanding of the framework's theories, practices, and tools, with a focus on recognizing personal patterns of stress activation and release. Every participant receives a certificate of completion along with training resources and materials based on the selected package.

Build capacity within your own team.

We recommend sending 1–3 educators from your school or district to become trained facilitators. Instead of bringing in another initiative that lives with a handful of people, you build shared language and capacity inside your own school community — educators with a common understanding of what is happening in the nervous system, what a behavior might be communicating, what a student or classroom needs right now, and just as importantly, what my nervous system needs.
